## 📌 Project Overview

The **Myntra Sales & Product Analysis Dashboard** is a business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, revenue, customer orders, product distribution, brand performance, and order status.

This project consists of two interactive dashboard pages:

**Part A — Sales Overview Dashboard:** Provides insights into total orders, revenue, order growth, city tier performance, category-wise orders, and brand-wise orders.

**Part B — Product Overview Dashboard:** Focuses on product distribution, gender-wise product analysis, brand performance, revenue by city tier, top 10 products, and order status distribution.

The main objective of this project is to transform sales data into meaningful visual insights that help understand business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses generate large amounts of sales and product data. Analyzing this data manually can make it difficult to identify top-performing brands, popular product categories, revenue trends, and order distribution.

**Objective:** To develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides a complete overview of Myntra's sales and product performance.

The project focuses on the following business questions:

- What is the total number of orders?
- What is the total revenue generated?
- How much has revenue and order performance grown compared to the previous month?
- Which city tier contributes the highest number of orders?
- Which product categories receive the highest orders?
- Which brands perform best in terms of orders?
- How are products distributed across different genders?
- Which city tier generates the highest revenue?
- Which are the top 10 products by revenue?
- What percentage of orders are shipped, cancelled, delivered, and returned?

## 🔍 Business Insights

Based on the values displayed in the dashboards:

### 1. City Tier Order Analysis

The order distribution across city tiers is:

| City Tier | Order Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Tier 1 | 37.36% |
| Tier 2 | 50.08% |
| Tier 3 | 12.55% |

**Insight:** Tier 2 contributes the highest percentage of orders, followed by Tier 1 and Tier 3.

### 2. Category-Wise Order Analysis

| Category | Orders |
|---|---:|
| School Shoes | 38K |
| Perfume | 23K |
| T-Shirts | 22K |
| Sneakers | 21K |
| Tops | 20K |
| Shirts | 18K |

**Insight:** School Shoes is the highest-performing category in terms of displayed orders.

### 3. Brand-Wise Order Analysis

| Brand | Orders |
|---|---:|
| Nike | 33.9K |
| HRX | 32.8K |
| Zara | 31.9K |
| US Polo | 30.9K |
| Puma | 30.3K |
| Roadster | 29.8K |
| Levis | 29.4K |

**Insight:** Nike has the highest displayed order count among the analyzed brands.

### 4. Product Distribution Analysis

| Gender | Products |
|---|---:|
| Men | 666 |
| Kids | 636 |
| Women | 601 |
| Beauty | 597 |

**Insight:** Men has the highest product count, while Beauty has the lowest product count among the four displayed groups.

### 5. City Tier Revenue Analysis

| City Tier | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Tier 2 | ₹10.31 Cr |
| Tier 1 | ₹7.74 Cr |
| Tier 3 | ₹2.59 Cr |

**Insight:** Tier 2 generates the highest displayed revenue, followed by Tier 1 and Tier 3.

### 6. Top 10 Products by Revenue

The dashboard displays the following leading products:

| Product | Total Orders | Total Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|
| Kids Wear | 4,308 | ₹0.30 Cr |
| School Shoes | 4,156 | ₹0.29 Cr |
| Jeans Product | 3,279 | ₹0.21 Cr |
| Lipstick Product | 3,169 | ₹0.21 Cr |
| Tops Product | 3,031 | ₹0.22 Cr |
| Face Wash | 2,842 | ₹0.20 Cr |
| Perfume Product | 2,579 | ₹0.16 Cr |

| T-Shirts Product | 1,991 | ₹0.12 Cr |
| Shirts Product | 1,978 | ₹0.15 Cr |

**Insight:** Kids Wear has the highest displayed revenue and order count among the listed products.

### 7. Order Status Analysis

The dashboard shows an almost equal distribution across four order statuses:

- Shipped: 25.06%
- Cancelled: 25.04%
- Delivered: 24.95%
- Returned: 24.95%

**Insight:** Shipped orders have the highest displayed share, while returned orders have the lowest.

### 8. Overall Business Insights

- Tier 2 contributes the highest order share and revenue.
- School Shoes is the leading category by orders.
- Nike leads the displayed brand-wise order comparison.
- Men has the highest product distribution.
- Kids Wear leads the displayed top products by revenue.
- Order statuses are distributed almost equally across all four categories.
